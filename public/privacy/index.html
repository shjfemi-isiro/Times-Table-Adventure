Privacy

For children

You can play without telling us anything. Your game stays on this device. A grown-up can choose to back it up. On leagues, other players only ever see your game name, like "Speedy Otter 27", and your avatar. Never your real name.

For grown-ups

What we keep, only if you sign in and turn backup on: your sign-in id from Clerk (our sign-in provider), your country, each player's avatar, game name, age band and, if you choose, a first name for the family board; each round's answers and times; consent receipts.

What we never collect: children's email addresses, surnames, dates of birth, photos, voice recordings, location beyond the country you choose, or advertising identifiers. There are no adverts, no chat and nothing for sale for money.

Where it lives: a Postgres database in London (Neon, AWS eu-west-2). The website and API run on Netlify. Sign-in runs on Clerk.

How long: round details for 12 months, weekly totals for 2 years, league tables for 12 weeks. A family inactive for 2 years is deleted. Full policy in the grown-ups' area.

Your rights: download everything or delete everything at any time from the grown-ups' area. Deletion is immediate and removes your players from league tables already published. To complain, use the form there; we acknowledge within 30 days. You can also contact the ICO (UK), NDPC (Nigeria), the Personal Data Protection Centre (Egypt) or the FTC (US).

Leagues are off until a grown-up turns them on and confirms by signing in again. Children aged 3 to 5 are never entered.
